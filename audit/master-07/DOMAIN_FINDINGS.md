# MASTER-07 domain findings

## SURGERY — 18 new IDs

Pediatric surgical workflow preserves procedure, provider, imaging, histology, infection, device and recovery provenance. Booking is not authorization for surgery; professional credentials and facility capability require source verification. Indications, anesthesia plans and complication response remain attributable clinical decisions.

- 1121: Qualifications, experience, specialization and languages remain separately sourced practitioner-role facts; a profile is not credential verification, active licensure or surgical privilege approval.
- 1122: Operating rooms, ICU, physicians and contacts are facility capability claims with source and validity; listing an ICU does not prove present staffing, bed availability or pediatric anesthesia coverage.
- 1123: Clinic, surgeon and telemedicine booking require appointment ownership comparison; a reserved slot does not establish surgical indication, guardian consent or emergency access.
- 1127: Inguinal, umbilical and diaphragmatic hernia remain distinct anatomical and urgency contexts; one registry must not imply an interchangeable operative pathway.
- 1130: Imaging, laboratory, recovery and complication AI tasks need separate intended-use evaluation, model/source versions and clinician disposition; no autonomous surgical monitoring response.
- 1133: Manufacturer and serial number remain exact implant provenance; missing serial identity must remain unknown and never be synthesized from a device class.
- 1139: Procedure, complication and recovery analytics require cohort denominator, follow-up completeness and missingness; AI forecasts are not causal estimates or individual treatment advice.

## ORTHOPEDICS — 24 new IDs

Pediatric musculoskeletal care preserves anatomy/laterality, growth and skeletal maturity, source measurements and functional baseline. Congenital, traumatic, genetic, infectious and oncologic profiles remain distinct under shared coordination. Neither imaging AI nor projected growth establishes indication or treatment; plans require qualified review.

- 4654: Hip measurements retain method, laterality, imaging modality and age context; developmental screening, conservative care and preservation surgery remain separately authorized steps.
- 4655: Curve measurements require source study, method, growth status and skeletal maturity; AI comparison cannot establish progression across incompatible acquisition methods.
- 4656: Limb length, rotation and mechanical-axis measurements retain units and anatomical frame; predicted growth is uncertain and cannot autonomously prescribe guided growth or lengthening.
- 4660: Growth plate location, injury classification, remaining growth and serial alignment remain explicit; apparent fracture healing does not close long-term growth-disturbance surveillance.
- 4664: Suspected and confirmed osteomyelitis, septic arthritis and implant infection remain distinct; culture/susceptibility and source timing inform clinician-led therapy without autonomous antimicrobial selection.
- 4665: Tumor anatomy, biopsy, histology/molecular diagnosis, resection, reconstruction and limb preservation retain source ownership; orthopedic planning does not independently restage oncology.
- 4667: Device recall support requires authoritative manufacturer/model/lot evidence and response acknowledgement. MRI-related context is not generic compatibility clearance; unknown lot must not yield a negative recall match.
- 4668: Prescription, fitting, growth adjustment, skin/comfort and functional goals are separate attributable events; an assistive-device record does not authorize replacement or change prescription.
- 4673: Guardian pain, cast, wound and adherence reports preserve timestamp and validation status; escalation needs a named recipient and fallback, without implying continuous monitoring.
- 4674: Adult handover and multilingual summaries reference verified current implant and medication records; school or cross-border access follows purpose-specific disclosure, not blanket passport permission.

## TRAUMA_REHAB — 23 new IDs

Trauma and recovery share a longitudinal episode with attributable source events rather than independent organ truth stores. Emergency action cannot wait for data completion. School/activity/sport clearances, home therapy and robotic rehabilitation require qualified clinical decisions; remote reporting does not establish staffed emergency coverage.

- 4677: One injury episode coordinates multisystem events while retaining each source clinician and time uncertainty; surgical prioritization is documented, never selected autonomously.
- 4682: Burn depth and extent retain assessment method/time and revision; fluid, airway and grafting entries document clinical decisions without generating unvalidated resuscitation instructions.
- 4684: Injury risk patterns require training-exposure denominator, calibration and uncertainty; low risk never constitutes injury exclusion or sports clearance.
- 4686: Exercise symptoms, syncope, family history and specialist restrictions retain provenance; automated normal findings cannot issue sports clearance or overwrite emergency plans.
- 4687: Growth and health take priority over performance. Menstrual, energy-availability and nutrition context requires appropriately restricted access; sharing sports participation does not authorize disclosure to coaches.
- 4688: Return-to-learn, daily activity, physical education, training and sport are separate versioned decisions with scope, restrictions, clinician and review date; one clearance cannot stand in for all five.
- 4692: Speech, language, alternative communication and swallowing/feeding safety are distinct therapy contexts; documented exercises cannot establish oral-feeding clearance.
- 4693: Instrumented kinematics, kinetics and muscle activity retain device, calibration, anatomical frame, units and acquisition context; motion comparison and digital-twin inference require compatibility checks.
- 4695: Exoskeleton/robotic prescription, settings, sessions, adverse events and maintenance remain separate lifecycle and encounter facts; data integration cannot program a device or autonomously change therapy intensity.
- 4697: Patient and guardian outcome reports retain respondent identity/role, instrument/version and context; proxy reports are not interchangeable with patient reports or imputed independence.
