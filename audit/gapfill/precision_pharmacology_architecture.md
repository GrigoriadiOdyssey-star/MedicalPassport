# Precision Pharmacology / Drug Intelligence
Revision 2026-09-14 · EXT-PHARM · DRAFT_SPECIFICATION · proposed owner CAP-CDS · shared clinical decision support / pharmacology / personalized therapy · roadmap RESEARCH, outside current P0.

## Purpose, scope and overlap
Provide a longitudinal, evidence-linked assessment of medication response, interactions, adverse effects, organ toxicity and alternative therapy scenarios. Existing sources already include pharmacogenomics, dose optimization, OTC/supplements, renal monitoring and therapy response: 1176, 1606, 1663, 1664, 1727, 1843, 2399, 2677, 4419, 4566, 5166. See [search dossier](new_module_proposals.json) and [pairwise preservation](semantic_diffs.json). Their missing common contract justifies an extension of CAP-CDS, not a duplicate source module. Do not build a prescribing engine or claim a simulated response is a measured outcome.

## Actors, permissions and workflow
A patient supplies reported exposure and symptoms; a treating clinician reconciles source records and reviews recommendations; a pharmacist reviews interactions; a qualified genetics reviewer adjudicates PGx interpretation. Research staff receive separate governed datasets. A service identity may read only the episode and purpose authorized by the consent/access service.
Workflow: reconcile medication history → freeze context → validate applicability and missingness → retrieve evidence → calculate an approved assessment/scenario → expose uncertainty and alternatives → clinician accepts, rejects or requests more evidence. An accepted recommendation still requires a separate authenticated medication order, with its own author and lifecycle. No AI or twin service may issue or change that order.

## Inputs and canonical ownership
CAP-MEDICATION owns medication statements, reconciliation and exposure episodes, with references to prescriptions owned by CAP-PRESCRIPTION, dispensing owned by CAP-PHARMACY and administration owned by CAP-ADMINISTRATION. CAP-DATA owns identity-linked clinical context; CAP-LABORATORY owns results and CAP-CDS owns derived assessments. CAP-AI owns model execution provenance, not medication truth.
The context snapshot includes age and date basis, sex-related clinical context where relevant, height, weight, BSA plus formula/input versions, diagnoses, chronic disease, allergies, prior adverse reactions, current and past medicines, active ingredients, strength/formulation, route, dose units, frequency, start/stop times and adherence uncertainty. Include OTC products and supplements, pregnancy/lactation state, pediatric age/development context, renal/hepatic function with equation and measurement provenance, biomarkers, devices, genome/PGx phenotype, available proteomics/metabolomics, clinical phenotype and observed treatment response. Unknown, not tested, absent and conflicting are separate values. Never infer normal organ function from an absent result.

## Data and interfaces
AssessmentRequest references patient, tenant, encounter, purpose, context snapshot/version, exposure IDs, evidence release and intended use. AssessmentResult references input hashes, findings, affected organs/pathways, uncertainty, applicability exclusions, missing data, model/rule version and clinician disposition. ScenarioComparison references alternatives and explicit assumptions; it does not create DoseChange.
Proposed internal commands: requestMedicationAssessment, compareTherapyScenarios, recordClinicianReview. These are contract proposals, not implemented API routes. Idempotency key prevents duplicate jobs; contextVersion mismatch returns STALE_CONTEXT; insufficient inputs return INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; authorization failure produces no clinical payload. Events AssessmentProduced, AssessmentInvalidated and ClinicianReviewRecorded contain opaque IDs and version metadata, not full patient records.
Candidate FHIR R4 mappings: MedicationStatement for reported use, MedicationRequest for an independently authorized order, Observation/DiagnosticReport for results, AllergyIntolerance, Provenance and Consent. Mapping profiles and partner terminology packs require separate validation. Dependencies: CAP-MEDICATION, CAP-LABORATORY, CAP-DATA, CAP-AI, CAP-TWIN, CAP-CONSENT.

## Evidence, AI and twin boundary
Knowledge AI supplies versioned evidence; Clinical AI explains an assessment; Personal AI provides an audience-appropriate explanation without prescribing; a twin consumes approved context to produce separately labelled scenarios. PGx knowledge must retain allele/phenotype interpretation, evidence source, version, jurisdiction and population applicability. CPIC distinguishes guidance on using available genetic results from decisions to order testing. The FDA association table itself does not imply that testing is recommended before prescribing. These are boundaries for the design, not a validated dosing rule set. [CPIC guidelines](https://cpicpgx.org/guidelines/) · [FDA table](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/precision-medicine/table-pharmacogenetic-associations).
Longitudinal response observations may support governed evaluation; they must not silently retrain a patient model or convert correlation into drug efficacy.

## Failure modes, privacy and observability
Conflicting exposure, stale labs, unsupported age group, missing units, uncertain PGx calls or model outage require explicit abstention or a limited result. Never invent dose values. Restrict genomic/family information, enforce purpose/jurisdiction and record source access, evidence release, reviewer action and invalidation without copying sensitive context into logs. Encryption, retention and revocation inherit the [data contract](data_architecture.md).

## Acceptance and unresolved gates
Tests must reject stale context, ambiguous units, unapproved models and autonomous dose/order mutations. A new renal result invalidates a dependent scenario; clinician review records the exact evidence/context versions. Both OTC and supplement exposures participate in interaction screening. Each finding opens its source and assumptions. Pediatric/BSA/pregnancy cases remain blocked until intended-use validation establishes eligibility. Open gates: validated formulas/thresholds, licensed knowledge packs, clinical evaluation, human factors and clinical governance. No live assessment or medical validation is claimed.
