# CORE-CAP-ENCOUNTER — consolidated source profiles

Extends existing canonical owner and all prior addenda. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## encounter-history

Encounter owns attributed procedure/treatment episode history with references to source-owned diagnoses, medication, imaging and documents. Specialty profiles retain procedure, complication, outcome and follow-up context.

Acceptance additions: Distinguish planned, performed, reported and amended procedures; retain operator, encounter, timing and source attribution. A registry entry does not authorize surgery or attest a completed intervention.
