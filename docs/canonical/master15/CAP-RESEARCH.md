# CORE-CAP-RESEARCH — consolidated source profiles

Extends existing canonical owner and all prior addenda. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## research

Research owns protocol/study/dataset provenance, discovery and candidate matching. Trial discovery is not recruitment, eligibility certification or consent. Preserve specialty and pediatric cohorts, phases, interventions and outcome requirements.

Acceptance additions: Verify purpose, consent/assent, protocol/version, jurisdiction, withdrawal and downstream dataset lineage. Candidate trial matches require qualified confirmation; study data is not automatically a clinical fact.

## research-datasets

Research dataset profiles retain domain-specific phenotypes, images, biomarkers, genomic context, treatment and outcomes. AI-ready means a source aspiration, not deidentification, consent or model fitness.

Acceptance additions: Dataset release requires purpose-limited access, lineage, revocation propagation and evaluation of reidentification and cohort limitations; clinical records remain separately authoritative.
