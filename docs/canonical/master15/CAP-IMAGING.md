# CORE-CAP-IMAGING — consolidated source profiles

Extends existing canonical owner and all prior addenda. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## imaging

Imaging owns acquisition/report references and longitudinal study linkage. Modality, anatomy, laterality, measurements, interpretation and derived AI artifacts retain source scope; imaging-derived recommendations belong to qualified CDS review.

Acceptance additions: Preserve modality, study/series references, acquisition time, laterality and interpreter attribution. Registration, segmentation, scores and AI image interpretation require separate validation; copied modality lists do not prove device interoperability.
