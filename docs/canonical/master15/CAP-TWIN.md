Extends existing canonical owner and all prior addenda. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## twin

Existing Digital Twin owns versioned scenario projections and their provenance. Organ/disease profiles supply distinct inputs and scenarios. Simulated, predicted and observed states remain separate; source treatment forecasts never authorize treatment.

Acceptance additions: Require scenario/model/input versions, uncertainty and applicability limits; reject stale or revoked inputs. Prediction errors cannot overwrite clinical records or trigger autonomous prescribing, ventilation or emergency disposition.
