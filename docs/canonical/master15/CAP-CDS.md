# CORE-CAP-CDS — consolidated source profiles

Extends existing canonical owner and all prior addenda. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## cds

CDS owns clinician-facing decision artifacts and their evidence/approval boundaries, delegating model execution to AI. Every specialty intended use remains separately scoped; predictions, treatment suggestions, eligibility and risk scores are not validated clinical decisions.

Acceptance additions: Require intended population/use, licensed evidence/model versions, uncertainty, clinician disposition and outcome-specific tests. Organ/embryo selection, mortality, antimicrobial, ventilation, pediatric and vaccine outputs stay blocked from autonomous execution.
