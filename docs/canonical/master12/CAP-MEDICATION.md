# CORE-CAP-MEDICATION — medication cluster addendum

Medication reference knowledge and reported-use chronology share the existing owner, retaining product/manufacturer/evidence version, batch/expiry, quantity units and reported-versus-observed use. Preserve official sheet content without converting it to validated clinical advice.

Earlier specifications and profiles are preserved. No new owner or implementation approval.

Original 406: Common/rare/serious and frequency claims retain evidence source, formulation and population; observed adverse events are separate from reference knowledge.

Original 407: Temperature/humidity/shelf-life instructions retain exact manufacturer/product/formulation release; no generic storage assumption after opening or excursion.

Original 408: Expiry observation retains packaging/source and date semantics; notifications do not authorize replacement, treatment interruption or disposal.

Original 409: Recall matches require authoritative notice/product/batch identity and update time. Missing batch is unknown, not a negative match; acknowledgement and clinical action are distinct.

Original 418: Daily/weekly/monthly adherence distinguishes reported, inferred, unknown and observed exposure; no moral label or inferred clinical effect.

Original 419: Treatment history preserves attributed start/stop/correction and reported-versus-prescribed/dispensed/administered distinctions; complete history is not guaranteed.

Original 420: Medication chronology references source-owned exposure and version/time rather than rewriting clinical facts; out-of-order corrections remain visible.

Original 422: Home quantity/consumption/low-stock tracking retains unit, product and source uncertainty; consumption estimates are not verified administration.

Original 423: Usage statistics preserve period, denominator, missingness and source type; counts of packages or reminders are not clinical adherence outcomes.

Original 429: Official indication/contraindication/dose/effect/storage sheets require exact product/source/version and jurisdiction. Displaying text does not validate treatment applicability.
