# CORE-CAP-CDS — consolidated source profiles

Uses an existing registered canonical owner and preserves all prior addenda. All owners already have canonical specifications. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## precision-clinical-recommendations

CDS owns patient-specific recommendation proposals, including clinical pathways, preventive screening, surgical planning, precision treatment and digital therapy proposals. Medication, genomic results and actual treatment remain their source owners. These distinct profiles do not constitute one approved algorithm or autonomous treatment authority. Pediatric proposals retain developmental stage and guardian/assent context.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every source clause with source-specific applicability, patient/tenant identity, evidence versions, uncertainty and correction lineage. Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Test wrong-patient, revoked consent, missing inputs, conflicting sources and downstream invalidation. Qualified clinical acceptance and actual service integration remain required.

## ai-clinical-decision-proposals

CDS owns explicit advisory diagnosis/ranking, guideline conflict handling, clinical prioritization and treatment/prevention proposals. Final safety validator and autonomous intelligence are source labels, not approval or autonomy. Rule execution yields a versioned proposal and reasons for review; actual prescribing, ventilation settings, disposition, orders and scheduling remain controlled by qualified workflow owners.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every source clause with source-specific applicability, patient/tenant identity, evidence versions, uncertainty and correction lineage. Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Test wrong-patient, revoked consent, missing inputs, conflicting sources and downstream invalidation. Qualified clinical acceptance and actual service integration remain required. Unknown/rare/unsupported cases must abstain and route to qualified review. Pin guideline jurisdiction/population/effective version, distinguish risk scores from triage authority, preserve unresolved contradictory rules, and test that no rule-chain or cached recommendation can issue an intervention.

## clinical-guideline-and-surgical-proposals
