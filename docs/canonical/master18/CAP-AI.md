## precision-derived-model-artifacts

AI owns derived forecasts, endocrine/oncology/genomic analytic artifacts and educational simulation outputs. Clinical-risk governance outputs are draft risk-register/corrective-action evidence for accountable human governance, not automated approval. Learning and surgical-skills simulations never confer credentials or act on a patient. Laboratory owns source results; CDS owns clinical recommendation acceptance.

Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Preserve germline versus somatic context, variant reference/interpretation versions and family disclosure boundaries. Claimed survival or reduced risk remains an unvalidated intended outcome.
