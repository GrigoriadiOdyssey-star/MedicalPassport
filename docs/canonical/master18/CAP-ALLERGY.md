## clinical-pediatric-hypersensitivity

Allergy owns attributed anaphylaxis, allergic-rhinitis, atopic-trajectory and diagnostic-assessment context. Laboratory owns IgE results and Encounter owns supervised challenges; association or sensitization does not automatically establish clinical allergy. Emergency recognition and epinephrine requirements preserve qualified response and actual administration as distinct states.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every source clause with source-specific applicability, patient/tenant identity, evidence versions, uncertainty and correction lineage. Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Test wrong-patient, revoked consent, missing inputs, conflicting sources and downstream invalidation. Qualified clinical acceptance and actual service integration remain required. Preserve age/development, body size and acquisition time where present, laterality/site, clinical phase, donor/recipient or mother/fetus identity, guardian/assent and adolescent confidentiality. No universal dose, threshold or protocol is inferred. Plans, actual procedures, device operation, clinical diagnoses and model suggestions stay separately attributable. Challenge-test and auto-injector training requirements cannot imply unsupervised exposure or automated dosing; preserve suspected/confirmed/unknown and reviewer evidence.
