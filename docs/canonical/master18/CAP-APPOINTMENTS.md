## ai-service-queue-and-scheduling

Appointments owns queue position, meeting/operating-room scheduling proposals and confirmations. Clinical priority comes from attributed clinical assessment; AI cannot infer permission to bypass emergency triage. Scheduling checks resource/team availability and concurrency; a proposal never claims a reserved room or accepted appointment.
