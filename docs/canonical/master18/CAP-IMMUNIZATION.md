## precision-vaccination-planner

Immunization owns vaccination-plan drafts conditioned on age, country, medical history and travel; plan is distinct from dose administration and specialist clearance.

Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Test wrong-patient, revoked consent, missing inputs, conflicting sources and downstream invalidation. Qualified clinical acceptance and actual service integration remain required.

## clinical-vaccination-history-and-response

Immunization owns distinct history, schedule, catch-up, response and vaccine-immune-care projections. Antibody/cellular laboratory results remain laboratory-owned; response is not automatically protective immunity. Plans, administration, contraindication, adverse events and certificates have separate state and qualified evidence.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every source clause with source-specific applicability, patient/tenant identity, evidence versions, uncertainty and correction lineage. Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Test wrong-patient, revoked consent, missing inputs, conflicting sources and downstream invalidation. Qualified clinical acceptance and actual service integration remain required. Preserve age/development, body size and acquisition time where present, laterality/site, clinical phase, donor/recipient or mother/fetus identity, guardian/assent and adolescent confidentiality. No universal dose, threshold or protocol is inferred. Plans, actual procedures, device operation, clinical diagnoses and model suggestions stay separately attributable. Pin schedule/product/antigen versions and jurisdiction; do not treat unspecified booster or adverse-event status as eligible, completed or caused by vaccination.
