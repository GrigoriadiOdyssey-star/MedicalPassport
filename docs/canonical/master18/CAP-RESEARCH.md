# CORE-CAP-RESEARCH — consolidated source profiles

Uses an existing registered canonical owner and preserves all prior addenda. All owners already have canonical specifications. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## precision-research

Research owns protocol-bound precision-medicine studies, biomarker validation and population analysis. Clinical patient data and study enrollment remain independently authorized; analytic evidence is not automatically validated or returned as clinical advice.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every source clause with source-specific applicability, patient/tenant identity, evidence versions, uncertainty and correction lineage. Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Test wrong-patient, revoked consent, missing inputs, conflicting sources and downstream invalidation. Qualified clinical acceptance and actual service integration remain required.

## ai-research-and-population-evidence

Research owns protocol-bound cohort, synthetic dataset, population surveillance and discovery artifacts. Policy/campaign suggestions and translational findings are proposals to accountable authorities. Synthetic data require disclosure/membership risk assessment; study associations, repurposing hypotheses and international statistics do not establish causation, approval or clinical eligibility.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every source clause with source-specific applicability, patient/tenant identity, evidence versions, uncertainty and correction lineage. Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Test wrong-patient, revoked consent, missing inputs, conflicting sources and downstream invalidation. Qualified clinical acceptance and actual service integration remain required. Record cohort/protocol/purpose, ascertainment windows, population denominators, linkage permissions, re-identification controls and controlled release. Research results must not silently populate patient advice.

## clinical-population-surveillance

Research owns population genomic and outbreak analytic projections. Contact-tracing and public-health actions need explicit authority and purpose beyond this registry; variant distribution and cluster detection retain denominator, geography/time, ascertainment and disclosure protections.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every source clause with source-specific applicability, patient/tenant identity, evidence versions, uncertainty and correction lineage. Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Test wrong-patient, revoked consent, missing inputs, conflicting sources and downstream invalidation. Qualified clinical acceptance and actual service integration remain required. Preserve age/development, body size and acquisition time where present, laterality/site, clinical phase, donor/recipient or mother/fetus identity, guardian/assent and adolescent confidentiality. No universal dose, threshold or protocol is inferred. Plans, actual procedures, device operation, clinical diagnoses and model suggestions stay separately attributable.
