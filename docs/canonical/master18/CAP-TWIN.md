## precision-counterfactual-simulation

Twin owns explicit disease and surgical-outcome scenarios distinct from observed patient state. Therapy comparison, complications and recovery are conditional simulations, not causal evidence or patient forecasts certified for use.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every source clause with source-specific applicability, patient/tenant identity, evidence versions, uncertainty and correction lineage. Unverified or stale context must remain explicit; never infer absent disease, permission or clinical eligibility. Test wrong-patient, revoked consent, missing inputs, conflicting sources and downstream invalidation. Qualified clinical acceptance and actual service integration remain required. Preserve assumptions, horizon, calibration population and model version; display uncertainty, unsupported scenarios and no-action status.

## ai-counterfactual-and-twin-state

Twin owns synchronized derived patient-state projections and conditional scenario comparison. Observed event timelines and simulated future milestones are visibly distinct, including population simulation. Validation engines produce evidence rather than self-approved model accuracy; no predicted response may overwrite observed outcome.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every source clause with source-specific applicability, patient/tenant identity, evidence versions, uncertainty and correction lineage.
