# CORE-CAP-AI: cross-jurisdiction guideline intelligence and surveillance

## Decision and scope

Extend existing Knowledge AI under CORE-CAP-AI with two distinct shared capability profiles: `GUIDELINE-CROSS-JURISDICTION` and `GUIDELINE-UPDATE-SURVEILLANCE`. One canonical Guideline Registry serves all specialties as projections. These are not new canonical owners, independent specialty document stores, AI gateways or prescribing engines. Existing evidence: CAP-AI anchors M08-CAP-AI-8604a804528d4840 (approved evidence), M08-CAP-AI-9d7d47ccc8e4d1dc (shared infrastructure), M08-CAP-AI-80b4b9dde1c5a0df (retrieval/provenance), and CAP-CDS M08-CAP-CDS-93599357d8174361 (versioned clinical evidence).

CAP-AI owns registry knowledge metadata, alignment, comparison and proposed semantic changes. CAP-DOCUMENTS stores immutable originals and separately identified translation derivatives; CAP-INTEROP owns source adapters; CAP-CDS owns clinical applicability/approval and downstream rule-release contracts. CAP-GOVERNANCE records accountable source/release approvals; CAP-SECURITY/CONSENT enforce access and patient-context permissions; CAP-AUDIT preserves decisions; CAP-NOTIFICATIONS delivers deduplicated notices; CAP-ENCOUNTER/PROFILE save authorized references. Telemedicine, Research, Precision Pharmacology (CAP-CDS) and Twin (CAP-TWIN) consume version-pinned references. Registry acceptance never grants prescribing or independently activates a model/rule release.

Status: DESIGN_DEFINED_RUNTIME_ACCEPTANCE_PENDING. No live official-source connectors, translation service, clinical comparator, polling worker or notification delivery is claimed. All 14 requested runtime acceptance criteria remain pending. The adult single-allergy MVP and its build gate are unchanged; this capability is a subsequent implementation wave unless explicitly selected later.

## Jurisdiction and matching

Capture PATIENT_HOME_JURISDICTION, PATIENT_CURRENT_LOCATION, TREATING_DOCTOR_JURISDICTION and CARE_DELIVERY_JURISDICTION independently, with country, subdivision/authority scope, origin, verification and nullable unknown value. Geography, citizenship, IP location and licensing are not interchangeable. The doctor selects applicable comparison jurisdictions manually, preserving actor/time/reason and the original context. A comparison choice does not assert a legal right to deliver care there.

Matching uses condition/diagnosis, age and unit/date, sex as clinically relevant and attributed, pregnancy status with explicit unknown, pediatric/adult context, severity, comorbidities, treatment stage, medication exposure, procedure and specialty. A title match is a candidate only. Preserve population exclusions, scope mismatches and unsupported contexts; abstain from a patient-specific match if mandatory context is missing. National, regional, professional-society and supranational guidance can coexist and conflict; do not silently collapse authority tiers or assume a higher tier is clinically superior.

Patient-specific relevance requires current patient consent AND verified clinician permission for that patient and purpose. Public-document discovery does not need patient data. Prefer local/minimized context mapping; never send patient identifiers or unnecessary health details to public repositories or external model providers. Revoked context access disables patient-linked comparison/impact views without destroying protected history. Outputs say “Applicable guideline section”, “Potentially relevant difference”, “Requires clinician review”; no diagnosis, treatment plan, medication, stop or dose mutation is emitted.

## Registry and immutable data model

GuidelineSource: source_id, source_type, issuing organization, country/jurisdiction scopes, authority_status, authority_evidence, specialty coverage, canonical URLs, connector_id/release, discovery_method (API/RSS/FEED/CONTROLLED_WEB/MANUAL), API/feed availability, monitoring permission/license evidence, schedule (DAILY/WEEKLY/MONTHLY/MANUAL/EVENT_DRIVEN), timezone, next_check, last_attempt, last_successful_check, last_changed_at, last_verified_at, failure_state and explicitly named approved secondary sources. Prefer API/feed; controlled page/document discovery requires technical/legal authorization. Schedule differs by source and risk; higher-priority medication/emergency/infectious-disease sources may poll more often. Monthly scheduling uses calendar dates, not an assumed number of seconds. Event-driven ingestion reconciles periodically under configured policy; manual mode has no background polling.

Guideline: guideline_id, country, jurisdiction, authority_id, specialty[], condition[], language, current_version reference (nullable), all_versions references, source_id, status projection, last_checked, next_check, translation_status, comparison_status and clinical_validation_status. A guideline can have scope-specific concurrent current versions; one registry must not imply one globally applicable document. An ambiguity prevents automated selection of a single current version.

GuidelineVersion: immutable version_id, guideline_id, issuer's source_version (nullable, never invented), title, specialty/condition/population scope, publication_date, effective_date (nullable), revision_date (nullable), source URL/reference, retrieved_at, original_language, original_document_reference, document_hash, source_authority_status_at_retrieval, evidence/grading_system (nullable), last_verified_at snapshot, supersedes_version_id, change_type and change_summary. Dates retain publisher precision/timezone and explicit missingness. Internal version_id is distinct from issuer version. Preserve original bytes and hashes, including replaced files at the same URL. Never rewrite old versions or delete them to simulate an update.

Lifecycle changes are append-only status assertions with actor/source/evidence/time. Current `status`, `last_verified_at` and `superseded_by_version_id` are materialized projections over those assertions, not edits to immutable version bytes. Statuses: CURRENT, SUPERSEDED, WITHDRAWN, ARCHIVED, PENDING_VERIFICATION; SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE is an availability overlay, not loss of known withdrawal/supersession. Retain history of linked corrections, approvals and supersession graphs; reject cycles, wrong guideline/scope and ambiguous branching. Withdrawn content remains available as clearly marked history and is excluded from automatic current recommendations.

## Authority, discovery and import

Prioritize national ministries, official national repositories, national medical authorities, recognized professional societies, official public-health institutions and relevant supranational authorities. An allowlisted domain is not enough: verify the issuer, document identity, publication context, scope and authority evidence. AI recognition alone does not confer OFFICIAL status. Store UNKNOWN, USER_SUPPLIED_SOURCE, VERIFIED_PRIMARY or VERIFIED_SECONDARY authority labels separately from clinical validation. Secondary sources require explicit identity/provenance and verification; an unofficial mirror never becomes authoritative by failover.

Unavailable source → SOURCE_TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE, last attempt/failure and last verified timestamp; keep the last verified bytes and known lifecycle state. Display that currentness cannot be reconfirmed. Network failure/404 alone is not proof of withdrawal. A successful unchanged check updates a verification assertion, not document version. Ambiguous metadata, language, date or scope stays pending. Preserve source license/access constraints: open the official original or permission-checked archived original; no claim that ingestion grants redistribution rights.

User-uploaded official-looking PDF/document starts USER_SUPPLIED_SOURCE and PENDING_VERIFICATION. CAP-DOCUMENTS quarantines it, validates type/size/archive depth and scans before extraction. OCR/AI may propose issuer, jurisdiction and version; human/source verification is required before linking to an official registry entry. Extracted text and embedded instructions are untrusted data, never tool commands. PDF retrieval follows an egress allowlist, redirect and internal-address checks, bounded size/time and content-type verification; source-controlled URLs cannot initiate arbitrary server requests.

## Update detection and validation

Each connector run is idempotent by source + document identity + content hash + observed metadata digest. Prefer conditional retrieval where supported, but ETag/Last-Modified are hints, not clinical hashes. A discovery or content change creates a candidate, verifies source/document, preserves bytes, extracts structure, and compares against the exact predecessor. Same bytes with changed official withdrawal, effective date or scope creates a metadata/status change record even without a new document hash. Different hashes do not by themselves prove clinical change: rendering/font/wrapper changes can be editorial. OCR ambiguity is UNKNOWN_CHANGE_PENDING_REVIEW, never automatically EDITORIAL_ONLY.

Pipeline: SOURCE_VALIDATION → DOCUMENT_VALIDATION → STRUCTURE_VALIDATION → SEMANTIC_DIFF → CLINICAL_CHANGE_CLASSIFICATION → SAFETY_REVIEW where applicable → PENDING_CLINICAL_REVIEW or APPROVED_FOR_KNOWLEDGE_LAYER. Every step records tool/model/release, input/output hashes, provenance and failure. Clinical significance is a reviewable claim, not a conclusion from a text-diff score. Numerical expressions, drug identity, units, negation, contraindications, evidence grade and applicability exclusions receive explicit alignment checks. A new candidate cannot silently replace verified knowledge while review is pending.

Detected changes include new guideline/version, revision, amendment, corrigendum, withdrawal, supersession, evidence grade, treatment/dose/contraindication/screening/monitoring and emergency criteria. Classifications: EDITORIAL_ONLY, EVIDENCE_UPDATE, DIAGNOSTIC_CHANGE, TREATMENT_CHANGE, MEDICATION_CHANGE, DOSE_CHANGE, CONTRAINDICATION_CHANGE, MONITORING_CHANGE, SCREENING_CHANGE, FOLLOW_UP_CHANGE, REFERRAL_CHANGE, EMERGENCY_CHANGE, LEGAL_OR_POLICY_CHANGE. Multiple classes may apply. A pending/unclassified change is retained rather than forced into this closed clinical classification.

Dose, contraindication, emergency, pregnancy, pediatrics, oncology and critical-care changes require qualified safety review. General clinically meaningful changes also require governed acceptance. Knowledge-layer approval makes a version eligible for retrieval with its scope/status, not active clinical rule execution. Every AI/CDS rule, Precision Pharmacology rule, Twin assumption and research release must separately pin and validate a new knowledge release. No automatic retraining, dose/order mutation or deployment follows a document update.

## Translation and comparison

TranslationArtifact pins source version_id/hash, original language, target supported EDHT language, translator/model version, translation version/hash, aligned source spans and review state. ORIGINAL_TEXT, MACHINE_TRANSLATION and HUMAN_VERIFIED_TRANSLATION are separate artifacts, never overwritten variants. Unsupported language returns an explicit unavailable result. Clinically important names, numbers/dose/lab units, terminology, evidence grades, contraindications, negation and legal warnings retain protected spans and undergo verification; a machine translation is not certified exact. Uncertain OCR/translation highlights the original and prevents unsupported comparison claims. Original text is always accessible through an authorized original link/view beside translation.

ComparisonArtifact is immutable and pins both guideline versions/hashes, jurisdiction selections, population/context snapshot (if permitted), aligned recommendation/section/page/paragraph anchors, translation versions, model/prompt/knowledge versions, retrieval/comparison times, consent/purpose and requesting actor. Missing anchors are explicit; the source can still open, but uncitable text cannot become a supported recommendation claim. Alignment covers Diagnosis, Investigations, First/Second-line therapy, Medication, Dose, Contraindications, Monitoring, Follow-up, Referral and Emergency criteria. Unmatched sections remain visible; absence is not agreement or proof of prohibition. Patient and doctor views apply different disclosure permissions to the same authorized comparison.

Each finding uses AGREEMENT, DIFFERENCE, ADDITIONAL_REQUIREMENT, CONTRAINDICATION_DIFFERENCE, DIAGNOSTIC_DIFFERENCE, TREATMENT_DIFFERENCE, DOSING_DIFFERENCE, MONITORING_DIFFERENCE, SCREENING_DIFFERENCE, FOLLOW_UP_DIFFERENCE, REFERRAL_DIFFERENCE or EVIDENCE_GRADE_DIFFERENCE. It contains Country A recommendation, Country B recommendation (nullable if not found), both available source anchors, publication/version dates, evidence level/grading system and limitations. Do not equate grades from different systems without a validated crosswalk; show the original grades. Conflicts and dated evidence are shown to the clinician, with no “best country” ranking or automatic superiority conclusion.

“Why do these guidelines differ?” returns only cited, supported explanations: publication timing, evidence base/grading, medication availability/formulary, population, care organization, screening policy or resources. Date differences are observable but not automatically a causal explanation for treatment differences. If authoritative sources do not establish a cause, return exactly “Reason not established from available authoritative sources.” Model plausibility is not evidence.

## Invalidation, impact and notification

Maintain reverse dependencies from exact version/translation/knowledge release to comparisons, saved encounter references, clinical rule releases, Precision Pharmacology, Twin assumptions and authorized patient/workflow references. Detecting a relevant update or status assertion immediately marks saved comparisons OUTDATED_COMPARISON (or UPDATE_PENDING_VERIFICATION if still uncertain), even before approving the candidate. Do not show the last approved version as unquestionably current when an official replacement/withdrawal is known. Old comparison remains immutable in audit history; request RE-COMPARE WITH CURRENT GUIDELINES as a new artifact referencing the old one.

When the source is superseded, old translations become SOURCE_SUPERSEDED through an overlay and a new version-pinned translation job is queued. Withdrawal and unresolved integrity failures also block their presentation as current. Never reuse translation bytes as a translation of another version. A source outage adds a freshness warning without inventing supersession. Approved same-document metadata changes still invalidate affected comparisons/assumptions where effective date, scope or status matters.

Impact records list affected canonical knowledge, Clinical AI rules, CDS logic, Precision Pharmacology rules, Twin assumptions, patient-specific comparisons and saved physician workflows, with dependency evidence and unknown coverage. Missing dependency inventory means impact unknown, not none. Recheck patient-specific impact only with current clinician authority and patient consent. Emit GUIDELINE_REVIEW_REQUIRED as a clinician task; do not change diagnosis, medication, dose or care plan. Source withdrawal may disable unsafe knowledge availability as a protective control; it cannot choose substitute treatment.

Notification levels: INFO, IMPORTANT, HIGH_CLINICAL_IMPACT, URGENT_SAFETY_CHANGE. Editorial-only updates default to history/digest, not interruptive clinical alerts. Clinical importance is evidence-backed and reviewer-governed; uncertain classification is labelled. The authenticated clinician view shows “Clinical guideline updated”, country/issuer/condition, old/new versions, effective date, source-cited changes, importance and permitted affected saved workflows/patients. Push/email contains only minimal metadata/deep links; reauthorize before displaying patient associations. Deduplicate recipient + guideline/version + notice kind; delivery/read/acknowledgement/clinical action are separate states. Configurable quiet/digest preferences do not suppress a required safety workflow without an approved escalation policy.

## Interfaces, events and operational controls

Versioned authenticated interfaces: GET sources/guidelines/versions/original references; POST candidate imports; POST compare; POST translate; POST recompare; GET aligned comparison/history/change records; POST clinician review; POST save-reference-to-encounter. Requests name explicit jurisdiction/context and immutable versions or an explicit “resolve current” operation. Return resolved versions and warnings. 400 invalid request, uniform 404 inaccessible/missing, 409 stale expected version/review, 422 unsupported context/language, 503 source/worker unavailable; no partial clinical conclusion on failure. These routes are proposed contracts, not deployed services.

Mutations require scoped actor/purpose, expected aggregate revision and tenant+actor+operation+idempotency key. Same key/same body returns existing result; changed body conflicts. Registry assertions, reverse-dependency invalidation and outbox commit atomically. At-least-once workers deduplicate event IDs/hashes; delayed events never restore revoked/current status. Events: GuidelineCandidateDetected, GuidelineVersionVerified, GuidelineStatusChanged, GuidelineChangeReviewed, KnowledgeReleaseApproved, TranslationSourceSuperseded, ComparisonInvalidated, GuidelineReviewRequired, GuidelineNoticeRequested and SourceHealthChanged. Events contain references/versions, not patient context or raw guideline text. Each consumer independently checks current permissions and validity.

Audit requester, four jurisdiction selections/manual override, guideline/translation/model versions, retrieval/comparison/detection timestamps, classifications, validation transitions and recorded clinician decision. Store physician note and exact selected document/comparison reference in Encounter/Profile under normal access rules. AI cannot write a clinician decision. Original clinical notes remain attributed to their author even when a cited comparison becomes stale.

Source health includes last successful check, overdue verification, backoff/rate-limit state, dead-letter queue, connector authentication expiry and allowed failover identity. Bound retries and concurrency; feed/web outages never delete artifacts or reset known WITHDRAWN state. Restore validates original hashes, immutable version/status history, dependency indexes and outbox positions before claiming currentness. Do not replay old “approved/current” events over later withdrawal. Polling SLAs, source-specific licenses, real credentials, load/fault evidence and accountable operational reviewers remain implementation/release requirements.
