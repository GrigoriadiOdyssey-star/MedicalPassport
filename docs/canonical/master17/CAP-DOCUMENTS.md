# CORE-CAP-DOCUMENTS — consolidated source profiles

Uses an existing registered canonical owner and preserves all prior addenda. All owners already have canonical specifications. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## document-translation-profiles

Documents owns immutable originals and attributed OCR/translation/verification derivatives. AI executes extraction/translation; Interop owns coded mappings. Medical translation artifacts and digital-signature checks are not independent confirmation of clinical truth, prescriber authority or accurate dose conversion.

Acceptance additions: Preserve original text/image, language, engine/version, source spans, reviewer disposition and corrected derivative lineage. Unknown tokens, negation, dose/unit and identity conflicts remain visible; no automatic prescription or diagnosis from OCR. Document trust, issuer identity, integrity and clinical verification are distinct states.

## ai-assisted-document-collaboration

Documents owns document version, collaborative review and signature provenance. AI drafts are unapproved derivatives; a collaboration message or model output cannot become the source clinician signature.

Acceptance additions: Preserve author/reviewer/signatory, immutable originals, version conflict and correction history. Reauthorize every collaborator and document retrieval; stale or revoked shares cannot reuse prior access, and AI cannot sign/publish itself.
