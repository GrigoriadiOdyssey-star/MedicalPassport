Uses an existing registered canonical owner and preserves all prior addenda. All owners already have canonical specifications. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## clinical-communication-profiles

Existing Telemedicine owns authorized remote clinical communication/session and case-discussion workflows, including translation and family-facing communication. Identity/Consent retain recipient authority; Documents retain signed artifacts, Encounter retains clinical decisions and Notifications retains delivery metadata.

Acceptance additions: Preserve participant/patient/case binding, jurisdiction and verified role, translation uncertainty and independent recording consent. Family membership gives no automatic access. Delivered chat or translated speech is not clinician acceptance or completed treatment; disconnects and revoked participation require explicit handling.
