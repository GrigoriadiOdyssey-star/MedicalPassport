# CORE-CAP-EMERGENCY — consolidated source profiles

Uses an existing registered canonical owner and preserves all prior addenda. All owners already have canonical specifications. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## registry-emergency

Emergency owns emergency/ICU workflow projections and handover context; Encounter owns episodes and clinical source owners retain authoritative observations. A registry view does not establish monitored coverage or clinical response.

Acceptance additions: Preserve source identity, source-specific fields, verification, chronology and correction lineage. Test unknown versus absent states, wrong-patient/tenant binding, stale or revoked access, conflicting reports and attributed human disposition. Clinical actions and sensitive pediatric/guardian disclosure require separately approved workflows; no inferred automation or compatibility approval.

## emergency-continuity-profiles

Emergency owns authorized emergency summaries, activation/continuity workflows and source-scoped emergency/ICU coordination views. Identity, QR, Profile, Notifications and clinical data owners retain their authority. Contact delivery or displayed hospital capacity does not establish staffed response, clinical acceptance or bed reservation.

Acceptance additions: Preserve source age, critical uncertainty, minimum disclosure and approved action-plan provenance. Distinguish activation, transmitted, acknowledged and attended states. Degraded/offline operation cannot promise current revocation or live coverage. No autonomous thrombolysis, airway procedure, antidote, rescue dose or seizure disposition is authorized by source workflow text.

## ai-assisted-emergency-coordination

Emergency owns source-scoped emergency workflow and cross-border coordination status. AI is an advisory dependency; triage, resuscitation, transport, receiving-provider acceptance and cross-border authorization require qualified actors and confirmed local arrangements.

Acceptance additions: Distinguish recommended, requested, acknowledged, accepted and completed transfer/intervention. Preserve uncertainty, location/source time, recipient authority and minimum disclosure; no implied international service coverage or autonomous emergency disposition.
