## hospital-notification-profile

Notifications owns authorized pre-arrival hospital message delivery and acknowledgement metadata. Emergency owns clinical coordination; transmitted data must be minimum necessary and permission-scoped. A sent message is not hospital acceptance or arrival.

Acceptance additions: Preserve recipient verification, payload version, consent basis, expiry and delivery/acknowledgement distinction. Test retries, duplicates, unverified endpoints and stale messages; do not infer staffed response from a delivery receipt.
