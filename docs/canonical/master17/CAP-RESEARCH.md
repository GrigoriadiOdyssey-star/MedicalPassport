# CORE-CAP-RESEARCH — consolidated source profiles

Uses an existing registered canonical owner and preserves all prior addenda. All owners already have canonical specifications. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## research-population-profiles

Existing Research owns research/planning and population-output profiles; AI executes models and existing Interop supplies source exchange. Protocol, cohort, study, geography/time and data-rights context remain source-scoped. Research and policy suggestions do not become approved clinical or public-health actions.

Acceptance additions: Preserve protocol/model/data/evidence versions, intended population, secondary-use permission, withdrawal and output-disclosure controls. Trial matching is not enrollment; population correlation is not individual diagnosis or causal evidence.

## registry-research

Research owns study, population-genomics and epidemiology registry projections. Cohort, purpose, protocol, geography/time, source versions and disclosure controls are retained; listing is neither enrollment nor clinical eligibility.

Acceptance additions: Preserve source identity, source-specific fields, verification, chronology and correction lineage. Test unknown versus absent states, wrong-patient/tenant binding, stale or revoked access, conflicting reports and attributed human disposition. Clinical actions and sensitive pediatric/guardian disclosure require separately approved workflows; no inferred automation or compatibility approval.

## precision-research-collaboration-profile

Research owns purpose-scoped precision-medicine collaboration among research centers, hospitals and genomic laboratories. Communication, laboratory results and clinical decisions stay with their existing owners; collaboration membership does not authorize cohort access.

Acceptance additions: Preserve study/protocol, data rights, genetic/family consent, permitted recipient and withdrawal lineage. No research collaboration automatically establishes clinical eligibility, diagnostic validity or approved therapy.
