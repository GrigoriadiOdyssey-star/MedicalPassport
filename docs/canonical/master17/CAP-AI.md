# CORE-CAP-AI — consolidated source profiles

Uses an existing registered canonical owner and preserves all prior addenda. All owners already have canonical specifications. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## ai-derived-analysis

Existing AI owns computation of attributed derived artifacts. Clinical scores/detections are advisory model outputs consumed by CDS; source records, clinical decisions, research releases and operational actions remain with their existing owners. Analytic family members retain distinct intended tasks and populations, not a single approved model.

Acceptance additions: Require source/task/model/evidence versions, data rights, uncertainty, abstention, bias/drift evaluation and correction/revocation propagation. No clinical exclusion, disease confirmation, staffing action or identity/permission inference follows automatically from prediction; emergency and pediatric outputs need task-specific safety validation.

## ai-knowledge-profiles

Existing AI owns versioned retrieval/indexing and model knowledge artifacts. Clinical evidence publishers, licensed terminology, regulatory sources and Research retain their authority. Retrieval does not establish current legal or clinical correctness; source updates are staged and reviewed, not blindly learned into treatment behavior.

Acceptance additions: Preserve source/license/effective dates, evidence grades and jurisdiction/population applicability. Verify citations, conflict handling, invalidation, rollback and prompt-injection boundaries. A knowledge-base record or named guideline cannot grant clinical approval.

## ai-lifecycle-profiles

Existing AI owns model/task lifecycle, execution orchestration, quality and evaluation artifacts. Feedback, federated learning and retraining do not automatically authorize production model changes or clinical reuse. Domain authorities and data/consent rights remain independent of model infrastructure.

Acceptance additions: Pin model, dataset, task and evaluation versions; preserve approval state, cohort performance, uncertainty and rollback lineage. Reject unauthorized inputs, unapproved retraining and misleading confidence. Numerical confidence is not calibration proof or clinical safety acceptance.

## ai-assistance-profiles

Existing AI owns source-scoped conversational/educational assistance and derived summaries. Clinical advice remains governed by CDS and clinicians; notifications, documents and communication have their own owners. Family-facing educational access does not imply access to confidential adolescent data.

Acceptance additions: Require attributed sources, explicit uncertainty, bounded context permissions, prompt-injection controls and no clinical-fact writeback. Do not diagnose, prescribe or interpret missing information as reassuring; escalation guidance requires separately qualified intended-use validation.

## operational-ai-proposals

AI owns draft workflow/network optimization artifacts, not hospital staffing or permissions. Clinical and Operations owners retain actual workload, shift and assignment decisions; network inference cannot establish professional credentials or patient access.

Acceptance additions: Retain task/data/model/version, constraints, uncertainty and authorized human disposition. Do not infer protected attributes, optimize away care obligations, automatically redistribute patients or update staff assignments from an unapproved model proposal.
