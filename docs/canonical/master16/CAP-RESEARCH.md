# CORE-CAP-RESEARCH — consolidated source profiles

Uses an existing registered canonical owner and preserves all prior addenda. For Pharmacy this is the first canonical specification under the already registered owner, not a new architecture. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## research-registries

Extend existing Research study registry profiles with protocol, cohort, research opportunity and project provenance. Retain pediatric, neonatal, reproductive, genomic, emergency and ICU contexts. Trial listings, observational registries and research recruitment are distinct lifecycle states; no additional service or consent is inferred.

Acceptance additions: Retain protocol/version, study identifier, recruitment status, population, permitted purpose and withdrawal lineage. Research availability is not clinical eligibility or enrollment. Preserve sensitive cohort and familial data permissions and prevent clinical reuse of unverified study observations.

## research-decision-profiles

Existing Research owns evidence-linked epidemiological and research-planning decision artifacts. Population risk maps and study/protocol recommendations remain scoped research outputs; they do not authorize clinical treatment, public-health enforcement or recruitment.

Acceptance additions: Retain population, time/region, provenance, data rights, protocol/model versions and uncertainty. Require population-bias/disclosure review and qualified confirmation before policy or protocol use; research recommendations cannot write patient clinical facts.
