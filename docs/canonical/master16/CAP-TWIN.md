# CORE-CAP-TWIN — consolidated source profiles

Uses an existing registered canonical owner and preserves all prior addenda. For Pharmacy this is the first canonical specification under the already registered owner, not a new architecture. Every clause below applies only to its listed source profiles; it is not a universal clinical rule. No new service, clinical approval or implementation readiness.

## neuro-oncology-twin-profile

Existing Twin owns the source-scoped neuro-oncology longitudinal representation and scenario provenance. Tumor passport projections reference source-owned pathology, imaging, treatments and functional outcomes; integrated diagnosis is clinician-attributed, not inferred from aggregation. AI summaries and late-effect predictions remain separate derived artifacts.

Acceptance additions: Preserve tumor anatomy/molecular classification, radiation exposure, implanted devices, neurological baseline, surveillance and survivorship variants. Distinguish observed, clinician-assessed, inferred and simulated states; version corrections and permissions must invalidate dependent projections. No tumor recurrence or treatment eligibility is established by the Twin.

## Source scope

- Original 5149 / neuro-oncology-twin-profile: Pediatric Neuro-Oncology Digital Twin, Tumor Passport & AI Intelligence Module — Represent CNS tumor biology, anatomy, treatment and functional consequences longitudinally within the EDHT Digital Twin.
