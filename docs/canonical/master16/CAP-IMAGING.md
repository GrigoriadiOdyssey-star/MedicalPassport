## echocardiography-records

Existing Imaging owns attributed echocardiography studies, measurements and reports. Longitudinal comparisons retain acquisition and interpreter context; management in this title does not transfer treatment authority.

Acceptance additions: Preserve study/series references, measurement units, acquisition time, report revisions and interpreter attribution. Prior images and AI-derived comparisons cannot substitute for a current qualified interpretation.
