## platform-governance-and-architecture

Existing registered Governance owns documentation, architecture decisions, product scope, clinical-governance/compliance evidence and release approval workflows. Technology names, platform-complete claims and regulation labels in legacy text are requirements/proposals, not proof of conformance or completed implementation. Medical audits are clinical-governance reviews distinct from immutable technical audit storage. Human accountable authorities approve policies; AI cannot certify law, licensure or clinical safety. No new architectural owner is created.

Acceptance additions: Preserve every original clause, patient/tenant/source/version/time provenance and explicit missingness. Test wrong identity, stale measurements, revoked consent, guardian changes, adolescent confidentiality and source corrections. Pediatric age, developmental stage, weight/height/unit/time and clinical context remain explicit where applicable. No automatic diagnosis, prescription, dose change, medication stop, device actuation or crisis disposition; qualified clinician review remains required. Clinical validation and production acceptance remain external gates.
