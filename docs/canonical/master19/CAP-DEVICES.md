Prescribing/fitting, caregiver competency and maintenance evidence are explicit workflow requirements, not inferred permission to adjust a ventilator or implant. Support plans and supply/power failure fallbacks retain qualified review.

No automatic diagnosis, prescription, dose change, medication stop, device actuation or crisis disposition; qualified clinician review remains required. Clinical validation and production acceptance remain external gates.
