## pediatric-vaccine-reminders-and-renal-escalation

Notifications owns authorized reminder/recall and renal escalation delivery with deduplication, acknowledgement and fallback. Clinical severity and required response come from qualified clinical owners; delivery is not clinician action, and alert fatigue controls may not silently suppress critical alerts.

No automatic diagnosis, prescription, dose change, medication stop, device actuation or crisis disposition; qualified clinician review remains required. Clinical validation and production acceptance remain external gates.
