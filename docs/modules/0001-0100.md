Направления проверки: ai-and-clinical-safety, clinical-intended-use, interoperability-and-jurisdiction.

### Project Overview

#### Purpose

Medical Passport is an international digital medical ecosystem that helps people safely travel with chronic diseases, prescription medicines and medical history.

The platform allows patients, doctors, pharmacies and emergency services to securely exchange medical information anywhere in the world.

---

#### Mission

Develop a portable medical information record. Acceptance depends on the receiving organisation, jurisdiction, verified identity, data provenance and supported interoperability profile; worldwide acceptance is an aspiration, not a present capability.

---

#### Vision

Become the global standard for secure medical information exchange.

---

#### Target Users

- Patients
- Travelers
- Doctors
- Pharmacies
- Hospitals
- Insurance Companies
- Emergency Services
- Family Members

---
