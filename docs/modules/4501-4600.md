Обязательное уточнение: [границы продукта](../product/product-scope.md), [безопасность](../safety/clinical-safety.md), [доступ к данным](../safety/privacy-and-consent.md). Направления проверки: minors-and-guardians, sensitive-data-segmentation, clinical-intended-use.

Purpose:
Prevent loss of clinically important information when pediatric renal patients transition into adult healthcare.

Core Functions:
- Transition Readiness
- Patient Education
- Self-management Skills
- Diagnosis History
- Kidney Function History
- Genetics
- Pathology
- Imaging
- Surgical History
- Dialysis History
- Transplant History
- Medication History
- Complication History
- Current Care Plan
- Adult Provider Handover

Lifelong Record:
Preserves the pediatric renal trajectory as part of the patient's permanent EDHT health history.

Transition Workflow:
Preparation → Readiness Assessment → Structured Summary → Adult Provider Transfer → Confirmation → Follow-up.

Medical Passport:
Critical renal and transplant information remains continuously available according to authorization rules.

Status:
Documentation
